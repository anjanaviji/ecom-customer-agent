# Cancellation Policy

## Overview

Customers can cancel an order before it has been shipped.

Once an order has shipped, it cannot normally be cancelled. The customer may instead request a return after delivery.

## Cancelling an Order

Customers can request cancellation from the Orders section of their account.

The cancellation request should include:

- Order ID
- Reason for cancellation

## Cancellation Before Shipment

If the order has not been shipped, the order will be cancelled and the customer will receive a full refund.

The refund is sent to the original payment method.

## Cancellation After Shipment

Orders that have already shipped cannot normally be cancelled.

The customer can refuse delivery or receive the order and initiate a return according to the Return Policy.

Return shipping charges may apply depending on the reason for the return.

## Cancellation of Part of an Order

Customers may request cancellation of individual items before the order is shipped.

If the remaining items are still eligible for shipping, the rest of the order will continue normally.

## Cancellation Due to Payment Failure

An order may be automatically cancelled if payment is not successfully completed within the required payment window.

No refund is required when payment was never successfully captured.

## Important

Cancellation is not guaranteed until the customer receives a cancellation confirmation.

If an order is already being prepared for shipment, the cancellation request may not be processed in time.
