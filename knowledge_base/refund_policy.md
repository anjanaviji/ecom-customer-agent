# Refund Policy

## Overview

Refunds are issued after an approved return has been received and inspected.

The refund is normally processed within 3 business days after the inspection is completed.

## Refund Method

Refunds are sent to the original payment method used for the order.

For example, if the customer paid using a credit card, the refund will be returned to the same credit card.

We cannot normally send a refund to a different payment method.

## Refund Processing Time

After the refund is approved:

- Credit cards: 3–7 business days
- Debit cards: 3–7 business days
- Bank transfers: 2–5 business days
- Store credit: Usually available within 24 hours

The actual time depends on the customer's bank or payment provider.

## Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be issued when:

- Only part of an order is returned.
- A product is missing an accessory.
- The customer received a damaged product but chooses to keep it.
- A promotional discount affects the refundable amount.

## Refund for Cancelled Orders

If an order is cancelled before shipment, the customer is normally eligible for a full refund.

If the order has already shipped, the customer may need to follow the return process instead.

## Refund Not Received

If the customer has not received a refund after the expected processing period, they should contact customer support with:

- Order ID
- Refund reference number, if available
- Original payment method

The support team can investigate the refund status.
