# Return Policy

## Overview

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

The product must be unused, in its original condition, and include all original packaging and accessories.

## Eligibility

A product is eligible for return when:

- The return request is submitted within 30 days of delivery.
- The product has not been used or damaged.
- Original packaging and accessories are available.
- The product is not marked as non-returnable.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following products cannot normally be returned:

- Personalized products
- Digital products
- Gift cards
- Products marked as final sale
- Products that have been used or damaged by the customer

## How to Request a Return

Customers can request a return through the Orders section of their account.

The customer should provide:

- Order ID
- Product name
- Reason for return
- Photos of the product if it is damaged

After the request is submitted, the return team reviews it within 2 business days.

## Return Shipping

If the product is defective, damaged during delivery, or the wrong product was shipped, return shipping is free.

For other returns, the customer may be responsible for return shipping costs.

## Refund

Once the returned product is received and inspected, the refund is normally processed within 3 business days.

The refund is issued to the original payment method.

Banks and payment providers may require an additional 3–7 business days to make the refund available.

## Important

Returning a product does not automatically guarantee a refund. The product must satisfy the return eligibility requirements.
