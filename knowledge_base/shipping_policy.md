# Shipping Policy

## Overview

Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation.

Delivery time depends on the customer's location and selected shipping method.

## Shipping Methods

We currently offer the following shipping options:

### Standard Shipping

Estimated delivery time is 3–5 business days.

### Express Shipping

Estimated delivery time is 1–2 business days.

Express shipping is available only for selected locations.

## Shipping Cost

Standard shipping is free for orders above $50.

Orders below $50 may incur a standard shipping fee of $5.

Express shipping fees depend on the delivery location and are displayed during checkout.

## Order Tracking

Once an order has been shipped, the customer receives a tracking number by email.

Customers can also view tracking information from the Orders section of their account.

## Delayed Orders

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed.

Orders may be delayed because of:

- Severe weather
- Public holidays
- Customs processing
- Incorrect delivery information
- Carrier delays

If an order has not arrived within 5 business days after the estimated delivery date, the customer should contact support.

## Incorrect Address

Customers should verify their delivery address before placing an order.

If an incorrect address is provided and the order has not shipped, contact customer support immediately.

Once the order has shipped, changing the delivery address may not be possible.

## Damaged Package

If a package arrives visibly damaged, customers should take photos of the package and contact support within 48 hours of delivery.
